About LS Developers

Initiated by Latif Sons.
Built on Twenty-Five Years of Trust.

For more than 25 years, our team has been building homes, developing plots, and delivering commercial and domestic construction projects across Pakistan. LS Developers was initiated as the construction and development arm of Latif Sons, bringing that same standard of precision into every project we take on.

Latif Sons itself is a long-standing name specialising in furniture, home décor, house mapping & surveying, and real estate — and is also the sole 1/1 authorised dealership of Molty Foam on Adiala Road. That heritage means LS Developers doesn't just build structures; it delivers fully finished, fully furnished living spaces, backed by a family business built over generations.

Mission

To design and construct residential and commercial spaces of lasting quality — delivered with honesty, precision, and full accountability to every client.

Vision

To be the most trusted name in premium construction and real estate development in the region, known for craftsmanship that stands for generations.

Core Values

Quality without compromise. Transparency with every client. Timely delivery. Respect for craft, from grey structure to the final finish.
